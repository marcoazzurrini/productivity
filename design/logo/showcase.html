Productivity

GTD desktop app · logo exploration · 6 directions

1. Clear Check

CAPTURE · DONE

A task box whose tick breaks out of the frame. The moment something leaves your head and stops nagging you.

2. Inbox Zero

CAPTURE · CLEAR MIND

An empty ring and one small thought arriving. Most of the mark is space: a clear mind, with room for what comes in.

3. Clarify

MANY INPUTS · ONE ACTION

Bars narrow down to a single point. Lots of loose stuff goes in; one concrete next action comes out.

4. One Day

THE WEEK · TODAY

Seven days in a row, with one standing tall. Your week, and the day you focus on. Echoes the calendar pane.

5. Done, Next

ENGAGE · MOMENTUM

A tick that turns into an arrow. Finish one action, move straight to the next.

6. The Split

THE CLARIFY DECISION

One path splits in two. The faint branch is ‘not actionable’, the solid one ends in an action. The core GTD question.
